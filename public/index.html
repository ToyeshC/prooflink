48-hour dispute window

      
All new attestations enter a 48-hour optimistic window. Validators stake SOL to vouch for a score. Challengers post a bond to dispute it. After 48 hours with no successful challenge, the attestation reaches economic finality — the stake is locked, the record is permanent.

How validation works

        

          

            
01 / Stake

            
Validators stake SOL to back an attestation score. Higher stake = stronger signal.

          

          

            
02 / Challenge

            
Challengers post a bond to dispute. If upheld, challenger earns the validator's stake.

          

          

            
03 / Finality

            
After 48 hours with no successful challenge, attestation reaches economic finality.
